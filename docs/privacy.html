Privacy Policy

Look Outside Reminder · Last updated 29 September 2026

In short: Look Outside Reminder does not collect, send, or share any personal information. It makes no network connections, has no analytics or tracking, and needs no account. Everything it uses stays on your computer.

What the app reads on your computer

To decide when to remind you to take an eye break, the app reads a few things locally. None of this leaves your device.

  • How long since you last used the keyboard or mouse. The app asks Windows for the idle time so it can reset the timer when you step away. It sees only that duration, never what you type or where you click.
  • When your computer locks, sleeps, or resumes. This is used to restart the work period.
  • Which apps recently used your microphone or camera. So that a break never interrupts a call, the app checks the record Windows keeps of which apps have used the microphone or camera and when (the same information shown under Windows Settings › Privacy & security). The app never turns on, listens to, or records from your microphone or camera itself. You can turn this off with the “Pause while I am on a call” setting.

What the app stores

Your settings, such as break lengths and preferences, and a short list of recently shown quotes (so they don't repeat) are saved in a settings file on your computer. Nothing is stored anywhere else. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes this file.

What the app shares

Nothing. The app does not transmit any data to the developer or to any third party.

The Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about the installation and any crashes, as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer only sees aggregated, anonymous reports that Microsoft provides.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the date above changed.

Contact
